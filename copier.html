❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I be both Provider and Investor?

Yes. Every TradeGuard account includes both a Master EA and a Client EA. You can share your strategy while also following other providers.

Can I stop copy trading anytime?

Yes. You remain in full control of your account and can disconnect your Client EA whenever you choose.

Does every connection require approval?

Yes. Copy trading starts only after both Provider and Investor approve the connection request.

Is my trading account secure?

Yes. TradeGuard only synchronizes trades between approved EAs. Your broker account always remains under your control.
